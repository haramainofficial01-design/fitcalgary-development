# Decisions

1. **Identity:** Keycloak V1, OIDC/OAuth 2.0 + PKCE, no Ory deployment. Provider integration is hidden behind an OIDC adapter.
2. **Web:** Native React/Vinext surface for public SEO/accessibility and admin density; all product data remains API-owned.
3. **Mobile:** Flutter for iOS/Android speed and parity; watchOS remains SwiftUI for platform-native behavior.
4. **Age rules:** labels are independent of non-overlapping configurable bounds. Seed assumptions are 18–22, 23–29, 30–39, 40+, with Open unbounded. Client confirmation is pending.
5. **Ties:** metric, then verification timestamp, then immutable result ID produces stable competition ranking.
6. **Pricing:** ongoing normalized monthly cost excludes one-time initiation fees; first-year normalized monthly cost shows them separately. Biweekly means 26 annual payments.
7. **Evidence:** default retention is 14 days after final review, configurable; pending-review expiry is surfaced and delayed by policy rather than deleting evidence mid-review.
8. **Data:** demo records require `DEMO_DATA=true` and are never production truth.
9. **Visual identity:** preserve the supplied warm off-white, charcoal, muted coral, hard-rule editorial system; refine responsive spacing, hierarchy, accessibility, and native Apple materials.
